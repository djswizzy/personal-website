## Computational astrophysics

I run three-dimensional Fortran simulations of stellar collapse on distributed systems. I adapted numerical solvers to preserve the magnetic-field divergence constraint on Cartesian grids and wrote C++ linear algebra routines for neutrino flavor evolution. In Python, I analyze terabyte-scale outputs to measure turbulent energy and local fluid rotation. [See the research and figures]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).

**Methods and tools** · Magnetohydrodynamics, numerical PDEs and ODEs, MPI, OpenMP, NumPy, SciPy, mpi4py, HDF5, and scientific visualization.

## Machine learning and evaluation

At Atomionics I developed convolutional neural networks with attention to rank potential ore locations from two-dimensional raster data. I also built evaluation frameworks for predictive models and a geology assistant that retrieves documents from a vector database. [See the evaluation result]({{ '/companies/#atomionics' | relative_url }}).

**Methods and tools** · PyTorch, scikit-learn, attention mechanisms, model benchmarking, ROC-AUC, ranking lift, and retrieval-augmented generation.
